# Cakupan bab

- **Bab 1: Introducing Deep Learning: Why You Should Learn It** - Tujuan bab; Mengapa deep learning penting; Prasyarat dan pendekatan buku; Siklus eksperimen.
- **Bab 2: Fundamental Concepts: How Do Machines Learn?** - Machine learning dan deep learning; Supervised dan unsupervised learning; Parametrik dan nonparametrik; Predict, compare, learn.
- **Bab 3: Introduction to Neural Prediction: Forward Propagation** - Forward propagation; Banyak input dan output; Jaringan bertumpuk; NumPy dan vektorisasi.
- **Bab 4: Introduction to Neural Learning: Gradient Descent** - Predict, compare, learn; Turunan dan gradien; Learning rate; Memorization dan generalization.
- **Bab 5: Learning Multiple Weights at a Time: Generalizing Gradient Descent** - Gradien untuk banyak bobot; Credit assignment; Banyak input dan output; Interpretasi bobot.
- **Bab 6: Building Your First Deep Neural Network: Introduction to Backpropagation** - Representasi berlapis; Full, batch, dan stochastic gradient descent; Backpropagation; Nonlinearitas dan konflik.
- **Bab 7: How to Picture Neural Networks: In Your Head and on Paper** - Jaringan sebagai graf komputasi; Correlation summarization; Bentuk tensor; Batas visualisasi.
- **Bab 8: Learning Signal and Ignoring Noise: Regularization and Batching** - MNIST dan generalization; Overfitting; Early stopping; Dropout; Mini-batch.
- **Bab 9: Modeling Probabilities and Nonlinearities: Activation Functions** - Peran aktivasi; Hidden-layer activations; Output activations; Turunan dan loss.
- **Bab 10: Neural Learning about Edges and Corners: Convolutional Neural Networks** - Local connectivity; Weight sharing; Bentuk keluaran; Belajar kernel.
- **Bab 11: Neural Networks that Understand Language: Word Embeddings** - Representasi bahasa; Bag of words; Embedding; Negative sampling dan analogi.
- **Bab 12: Neural Networks that Write Like Shakespeare: Recurrent Layers** - Data dengan panjang berubah; Bag of words vs. recurrence; Backpropagation through time; Perplexity.
- **Bab 13: Introducing Automatic Optimization: Building a Deep Learning Framework** - Tensor dan graf komputasi; Autograd; Layer, loss, optimizer; Embedding dan recurrence.
- **Bab 14: Learning to Write Like Shakespeare: Long Short-Term Memory** - Character language modeling; Truncated BPTT; Vanishing dan exploding gradients; LSTM gates; Evaluasi bahasa.
- **Bab 15: Deep Learning on Unseen Data: Introducing Federated Learning** - Masalah privasi; Federated averaging; Ancaman terhadap pembaruan; Secure aggregation; Homomorphic encryption.
- **Bab 16: Where to Go from Here: A Brief Guide** - Berpindah ke framework; Belajar berkelanjutan; Portofolio dan komunikasi; Komputasi dan komunitas; Tanggung jawab.
